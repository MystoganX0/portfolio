About Me.

I am an Information System Engineering student currently finalizing my academic journey through a comprehensive Final Year Project. My primary technical focus is full-stack web development, where I have sustained, hands-on experience building prototyping systems, fixing database integrations, and executing seamless system migrations. I frequently work within the PHP ecosystem, confidently handling environment upgrades from PHP 7.4 to 8.3, and I utilize MySQL alongside Tailwind CSS to create robust, visually compelling digital solutions.

Beyond coding for the web, I am deeply engaged in system analysis and design. I regularly translate complex business requirements into actionable architecture using UML diagrams, Entity Relationship Diagrams (ERD), and Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN). A recent practical application of this was a comprehensive driving school management system I designed for a client in Shah Alam, mapping out precise "As-Is" versus "To-Be" operational processes. Additionally, my analytical interests extend into data science and machine learning, where I build predictive models using algorithms like Random Forest, Naive Bayes, and Support Vector Machines to derive actionable insights from complex datasets.

My curiosity for technology also bridges software and hardware through DIY Internet of Things (IoT) prototyping, working closely with sensors and actuators in closed-loop systems to build functional physical products. Across all these domains, I maintain a strong, underlying commitment to technical ethics and cybersecurity, continuously analyzing the risks associated with cloud computing and data breaches to ensure the systems I engineer are both innovative and secure.
